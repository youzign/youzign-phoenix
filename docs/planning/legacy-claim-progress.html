Bottom line: NOT safe to announce yet. Import plumbing + data rescue are done, but ~1 in 3 sampled designs opens visibly wrong. The clipart-recolor bug and the font fallback account for most of it and look very fixable; the matrix-sliver and canvas-size cases need the saved designstrings above.
