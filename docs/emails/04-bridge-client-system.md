The reason brands hire is simple. They try to make the images themselves with AI, and the product comes out distorted. Proportions drift. It looks off. So they hire someone who can do it properly. That someone can be you.

I applied with one rule. Always send a sample first. Three sample images for their actual product, made before they asked, built in Dezygn in under 15 minutes. It worked. Small jobs at first, then retainers.

If you have ever sent proposals into a void and heard nothing back, this next part is for you.

Evidence beats a promise. If your proposals go unanswered, you are probably not losing on skill. You are losing before anyone reads a word. Leading with proof is the fix.
