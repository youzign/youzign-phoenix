# Email 3: Everything new in Youzign (follow-up)

Audience: ~20,000 legacy Youzign customers (Elastic Email), reusable for the new downloads audience
From: Bertrand <support@youzign.com>

---

## Subject line options (ranked)

1. **Everything that is new inside Youzign**
   Plain, exact, and it sets the expectation the whole email delivers on. Best for openers who already know the app is back.

2. **It is not the Youzign you remember (in a good way)**
   Curiosity plus a promise. Signals real change without hype, and the parenthetical keeps it warm.

3. **The new Youzign does things the old one never could**
   States the payoff directly. Good for readers who opened email 1 but have not downloaded yet.

4. **Background removal, AI images, PDF export: what is new in Youzign**
   Leads with three concrete features, so the scan-readers know exactly what they are getting.

5. **A quick tour of what changed inside Youzign**
   Low-pressure, invitational, reads like a note from a person. Softest of the five.

## Body (plain text)

Hi,

Last time I told you Youzign is back and free for everyone. This time I want to show you what is new inside it. It is a long list, so I will keep each line short.

The magic stuff:

- **Background removal.** Free, unlimited, runs on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Magic Eraser.** Brush over something and it is gone.
- **Magic Edit.** Brush an area, type what should be there instead.
- **Magic Grab.** Click a subject to lift it onto its own layer.
- **Magic Expand.** Extend a photo past its edges.
- **Upscale.** Turn a small image into a sharp large one.
- **AI image generation.** Bring your own AI key and make images at cost, no credit packs.

For everyday design:

- **13 new filters.** Plus sliders for brightness, contrast, saturation, warmth and vignette.
- **Brand Kit.** Save your logo, colors and fonts once.
- **Font combinations.** Ready-made pairings that already look good together.
- **Text effects.** Outline, neon, sticker, shadow and echo for headlines.
- **Thousands of icons.** Searchable, built right into the editor.
- **Photo library.** Free photos you can drop in without leaving the app.
- **Smart snap guides.** Things line up on their own as you move them.

Documents and export:

- **Multi-page documents.** Real pages with thumbnails you can drag to reorder.
- **PDF export.** Plus PNG and JPG up to 5x size.
- **Smart resize.** Switch to any format and your content scales to fit.
- **Autosave.** Your work saves as you go.

And the foundation:

- **Automatic updates.** A blue dot on the logo tells you a new version is ready.
- **Works offline.** No account, no server. It is open source and yours to keep.

Your old work is coming too. Archive import from the original youzign.com is almost ready, so the designs you made years ago will open in the new app.

Download it here:

https://www.youzign.com

Already installed? The blue dot on the logo gets you the newest version.

Bertrand
Founder, Youzign
