From: Bertrand <support@youzign.com>

## Subject line options (ranked)

1. **The design editor you bought for life is back. Free, for everyone.**
   Leads with Bertrand's own core line, states the news plainly, and the word "life" pulls the exact right memory for a lifetime-deal buyer.

2. **A promise kept, 12 years later**
   The landing-page kicker verbatim. Short, personal, high curiosity. Reads like a note from a person, not a product blast. Strong for openers who trust the sender.

3. **You bought Youzign for life. Here it is.**
   Blunt and warm. Frames the whole thing as returning something that was theirs, not launching something new.

4. **I rebuilt Youzign and gave it away, free forever**
   First-person and honest about what actually happened. Good if we want the founder-confession angle to lead.

5. **Youzign is back (and it is finally yours to keep)**
   Softer, curiosity-forward, echoes "the app is yours to keep."

## Body (plain text)

Hi,

Years ago you bought Youzign. It was sold as a lifetime deal, and then, at some point, the product went away. That was not okay, and I have thought about it more than you probably realise.

So here is what I did about it.

I rebuilt Youzign from scratch. It is the design editor you bought for life, now free for everyone, forever. A promise kept, 12 years later.

It is completely different underneath and completely familiar to use. This time it is an open-source desktop app for Mac, Windows and Linux. No subscriptions, no servers, no accounts. Your designs live on your computer, and the app is yours to keep. That is what lifetime should have meant all along.

What that means in practice:

- Free for life. Actually. No tiers, no trials, no catch.
- Open source. You can inspect it, fork it, trust it. It cannot be taken away again.
- It works offline and local-first. Your designs stay on your machine, and you can export them any time.
- No account to create. Download it, open it, start designing.
- It updates itself. When a new version is out you will see a blue dot on the logo inside the app. Your designs stay exactly where they are.

Download it here:

https://www.youzign.com

One more thing worth knowing: I am working on bringing your old designs across too. Archive import from the original youzign.com is landing soon, so the work you made years ago will open in the new app. Nothing you made is being held hostage.

Youzign has been loved by more than 20,000 designers since 2014. If you were one of them, this is me making good on what you paid for. Go download it. It is yours.

Thank you for backing it all those years ago.

Bertrand
Founder, Youzign

Questions? Just reply, or email support@youzign.com
